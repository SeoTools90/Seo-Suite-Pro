Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. What is a Robots.txt file?

A Robots.txt file tells search engine crawlers which pages or files they can or cannot request from your site.

2. Where should I upload the Robots.txt file?

Upload it directly to the root directory of your website (e.g., yourdomain.com/robots.txt).

3. Will a Robots.txt file hide my site from Google?

It instructs crawlers not to crawl blocked pages, but shouldn't be used as a security mechanism for private content.
