Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. How is typing speed (WPM) calculated?

Words Per Minute (WPM) is calculated by taking the number of correctly typed words divided by the total time spent typing in minutes.

2. What is a good typing speed for professionals?

An average typing speed is around 40 WPM. Professional typists, programmers, and content writers usually aim for speeds between 60 WPM to 80+ WPM with 95%+ accuracy.

3. How do the Basic, Intermediate, and Advanced levels differ?

Basic level uses short, simple words. Intermediate introduces complex sentences and common vocabulary, while Advanced incorporates numbers, punctuation, and challenging terminology.
