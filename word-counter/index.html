Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. Why is word count important for SEO?

Word count plays a vital role in content ranking. Detailed, well-researched content that thoroughly answers user queries tends to rank higher on search engines like Google.

2. How does the Word & Character Counter work?

Simply type or paste your text into the box above. The tool automatically calculates the total number of words, characters, sentences, and estimated reading time in real-time.

3. Is my text saved on any server?

No, your privacy is completely safe. All calculations happen client-side in your browser, and no data is stored or transmitted anywhere.
