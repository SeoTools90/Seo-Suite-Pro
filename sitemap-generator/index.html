Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. What is an XML Sitemap?

An XML Sitemap is a roadmap of your website that leads search engines to all your important pages. It allows crawlers to index your site faster and more efficiently.

2. How do I submit my sitemap to Google Search Console?

Copy the URL of your sitemap (e.g., yourdomain.com/sitemap.xml), log in to Google Search Console, navigate to the "Sitemaps" section, paste the link, and click Submit.

3. How often should I update my sitemap?

You should regenerate and update your sitemap whenever you add new pages, blog posts, or make significant structural changes to your site.
