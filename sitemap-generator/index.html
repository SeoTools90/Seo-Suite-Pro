Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. What is an XML Sitemap?

An XML Sitemap is a roadmap of your website that leads search engines to all your important pages.

2. How do I submit my sitemap to Google Search Console?

Copy your sitemap URL, log in to Google Search Console, navigate to "Sitemaps", paste the link, and click Submit.

3. How often should I update my sitemap?

You should regenerate and update your sitemap whenever you add new pages or blog posts.
