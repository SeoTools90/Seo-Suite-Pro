Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. Can I embed my brand logo inside the QR code?

Yes! You can upload any image or PNG logo using the "Center Logo / Image" option. The generator will position it automatically without breaking scan accuracy.

2. Are custom-colored QR codes scan-friendly?

Yes, as long as there is high contrast between the dots color and background color. For best performance, keep the dots color darker than the background color.

3. Do these generated QR codes expire?

No! The QR codes generated by this tool are static and permanent. They will work indefinitely as long as your destination URL remains active.
