Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. What are Meta Tags and why are they important?

Meta tags are snippets of text that describe a page's content in the site's HTML code. They help search engines like Google understand your webpage and improve SEO rankings.

2. How do I use this Meta Tag Generator?

Simply enter your website title, description, and target keywords into the form fields and click "Generate". Copy the generated HTML code into the <head> section of your website.
